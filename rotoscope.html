Rotoscope project progress

Weekly milestones from references through polish.

This page documents rotoscope production across five weeks: planning, tracing, cleanup, color and compositing, then timing and export tests.

Week 1: Planning and references

Selected footage, isolated motion beats worth tracing, and drafted a manageable shot count for the first pass.

Week 2: Rough tracing

Drew primary silhouettes on key frames and aligned poses with playback timing.

Week 3: Cleanup

Normalized line weight, reduced jitter between frames, and corrected proportional drift.

Week 4: Color and compositing

Applied flat fills, split foreground and background layers, and tested blend modes.

Week 5: Timing and exports

Adjusted rhythm between holds and moves, then exported previews for review.
