Weekly progress log

  1. Week 1 — Reference & plate

    Locked camera angle; gathered HD plate.

    Trimmed handles, noted motion peaks for the gesture we are tracing. No paint yet.

  2. Week 2 — Rough keys

    Every fourth frame blocked.

    Loose line on 4s; silhouette read first. Timing matched to audio scratch.

  3. Week 3 — Inbetweens

    Filled gaps on 2s.

    Smoothed arcs; corrected one drift on the forearm. Ready for tie-down pass.

  4. Week 4 — Tie-down & clean-up

    Single consistent contour.

    Closed gaps, aligned line ends, removed doubling from multi-pass scans.

  5. Week 5 — Flat colour

    Base fills under line.

    Palette restricted to five swatches; flats keyed to story beat (warm shift mid-shot).

  6. Week 6 — Finish & export

    Minimal shadow pass; ProRes master.

    Edge softening on two frames only; grain matched to plate. Sequence rendered for critique.
