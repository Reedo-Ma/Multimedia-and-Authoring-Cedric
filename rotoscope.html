Rotoscope Project Progress

This log records the development of a rotoscope sequence from concept to refinement. Each week highlights what was completed and what remains.

Weekly Breakdown

Week 1: Planning and Reference Collection

Selected source footage, identified key motion segments, and prepared a shot list for a manageable first pass.

Week 2: Initial Frame Tracing

Completed rough outlines on primary character movement with attention to timing and silhouette consistency.

Week 3: Cleanup and Line Consistency

Refined line quality, corrected proportion drift between frames, and standardized stroke weight for visual coherence.

Week 4: Color and Compositing

Applied flat color passes, separated layers for foreground and background, and tested compositing options.

Week 5: Timing Adjustments and Export Tests

Tuned playback rhythm, removed jitter in transitional frames, and generated preview exports for review.
